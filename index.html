GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS?
WE GOT YOU.

A few quick answers before game time.

How do I book a slot?+

Tap any Book on WhatsApp button and tell us your sport, date and preferred time. Our team will confirm availability in chat.

What are the rates?+

Rates can depend on the sport, day and time. Send us a message on WhatsApp for the current rates.

How long is a slot?+

Slots are typically one hour. Ask us about consecutive hours if you’re planning a longer game.

Should I book in advance?+

Booking in advance is a good idea, especially for evenings and weekends.

Can I change my booking?+

Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can to discuss changes and availability.
